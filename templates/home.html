Challenge Information

NASA missions like Kepler, TESS and K2 have discovered so much outside of our solar system. However, identifying these objects is slow, requiring scientists to sift through mountains of data. We are changing this: training an ML model on publicly available datasets, we make exoplanet identification and classification accessible and efficient to everybody.
